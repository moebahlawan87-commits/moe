Privacy Policy — QR Code Generator

Last updated: 23 May 2026

What this app collects

QR Code Generator does not collect any personal information from you. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

What you enter into the app

You enter text (for example a URL, a phone number, a Wi-Fi password, or any plain text) and the app generates a QR code image from it locally on your device. The text you enter and the resulting image stay on your device — they are never sent to any server we control.

Network connections

The app uses the internet only to load and show ads (see "Advertising" below). Nothing you enter into the app is sent over the network.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — required to load ads.

The app does not request the camera, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission. The app generates QR codes from text you type; it does not scan QR codes through the camera.

Advertising

QR Code Generator shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDKs may collect and process limited data (for example, your device's advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location) to serve ads and measure ad performance. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a Google-provided consent form (UMP — User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can choose whether ads are personalized.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a "family" app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
