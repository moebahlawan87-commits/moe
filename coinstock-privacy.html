CoinStock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

CoinStock ("the app") is a personal crypto & stock portfolio tracker published by DevMR. In short: your portfolio is stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

Information you create

The holdings, quantities, average costs and watchlist you enter are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used to load live market prices, advertisements, and the consent form. CoinStock requires a connection to show up-to-date prices.
  • Notifications — used only to deliver the price alerts you set yourself. You can decline this permission or turn alerts off at any time, and the app works fully without it.

Price alerts

If you set a price alert, CoinStock periodically checks the live price of that asset in the background and shows a notification on your device when your target is reached. The target price and chosen asset are stored only on your device; nothing about your alerts is sent to us or any server.

Market data

To display live prices, CoinStock requests public, read-only market data from CoinGecko (cryptocurrencies) and Yahoo Finance (stocks & ETFs). Only the asset symbols you track are sent in these requests. No personal information, holdings amounts, or identifiers are included.

Not financial advice

CoinStock is an informational tracking tool only. Prices may be delayed or inaccurate and nothing in the app is investment advice. Always do your own research before making financial decisions.

Advertising

CoinStock shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), CoinStock uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via More → Privacy & ad choices.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

CoinStock is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
