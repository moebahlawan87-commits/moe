What this app is (and is not)

CargoLink is an independent directory and shipping helper that gathers the world’s most well-known cargo, courier, postal, freight-forwarding, ocean-line and air-cargo companies in one place and helps you reach them quickly. The app adds its own native features: a step-by-step origin→destination shipping wizard that suggests carriers for your chosen lane, a searchable carrier directory with category filters and on-device starred favourites, a universal tracking entry that hands your tracking number to the carrier’s own tracking page, a side-by-side carrier comparison, and an offline shipping guide. When you open a carrier, its own public website loads inside a standard in-app browser, and you can hand the page off to your phone’s default browser at any time with “Open in browser”. How the in-app browser works

Tapping a carrier — to open its website to ship, or to track a parcel — opens that carrier’s own public website inside Android’s standard WebView component, scoped to the URL you selected. The page is served directly by the carrier — the same content you would get by typing the address into any browser. CargoLink does not inject scripts into those pages, and does not read your cookies, login credentials, session tokens, or the content you submit on them. For sign-in, booking, or any flow involving payment, we recommend using the “Open in browser” button so the flow runs in your full system browser, where the third-party site’s own privacy policy applies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

CargoLink shows ads to keep the app free. Ads are served by Google’s AdMob SDK, which is integrated as a library inside the app. When AdMob is active it may collect or use certain device-level information, depending on whether you consent (in the EEA / UK / Switzerland) and on Google’s own policies:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable, device-level identifier you can change at any time in Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • App interactions used to limit ad frequency and measure ad performance.

The app does not perform user tracking across other apps or websites that we own. To learn more or change your choices, please see Google’s privacy notice at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and the device-level controls under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, on first launch the app shows a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form so you can manage how AdMob personalizes ads. Your choice is stored locally by the UMP SDK and is respected by the app when loading ads. You can reopen these choices any time from the app’s “Privacy options” menu where required.

Children

CargoLink is a general-audience logistics directory and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and provided personal information through a third-party site you reached from the app, please contact that site directly under its own privacy policy.

Internet required

CargoLink needs an internet connection to load carrier pages and to keep the app free with ads. When offline, the app shows a “You’re offline” screen and does not enable the directory.
