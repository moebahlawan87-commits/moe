Privacy Policy — Qatar Jobs Compass

Last updated: 30 May 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is (and is not)

Qatar Jobs Compass is an independent directory that gathers the top trending Qatar job-search websites in one place and helps you reach them quickly. On top of that directory the app adds its own native features: a salary explorer with indicative monthly pay bands by sector, a plain-language “Working in Qatar” guide, and saved-site bookmarks. When you tap a job site, it opens inside a simple in-app browser, and you can hand the page off to your phone’s default browser at any time with “Open in browser”. Qatar Jobs Compass is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the websites or employers it links to (Bayt, GulfTalent, Naukrigulf, Indeed, Monster Gulf, Tanqeeb, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Qatar Living, Hukoomi, Qatar Airways, Hamad Medical Corporation, Qatar University, or any other). All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

How the in-app browser works

Tapping a job site opens that site’s own public website inside Android’s standard WebView component, scoped to the URL you selected. The page is served directly by the website — the same content you would get by typing the address into any browser. Qatar Jobs Compass does not inject scripts into those pages, and does not read your cookies, login credentials, session tokens, or the content you submit on them. For sign-in or applying with an account, we recommend using the “Open in browser” button so the flow runs in your full system browser, where the third-party site’s own privacy policy applies.

Salary & guide content

The salary bands and the “Working in Qatar” guide are general, informational estimates and orientation only. They are not an offer of employment, a guarantee of pay, or financial, legal, or immigration advice. Rules and figures change — always confirm current requirements with the Qatar Ministry of Labour (MADLSA) and official Qatar government sources before acting.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to load job-site pages and to request ads. The app shows a “You’re offline” screen when there is no connection.

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

Qatar Jobs Compass shows ads provided by Google AdMob in three formats: banner (a small strip at the bottom of the screen), interstitial (an occasional full-screen ad after several site opens), and app-open (a full-screen ad when you bring the app back to the foreground — never on the first launch). Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and you can re-open the consent form from About → “Ad & Privacy Choices”.

No fees, ever

Qatar Jobs Compass is free and will never ask you to pay for a job, a visa, or an application. Legitimate Qatar employers do not charge candidates. Treat anyone who asks for payment as a red flag.

Children

Qatar Jobs Compass is intended for job seekers and is not designed for children. The AdMob ad content is mixed-audience.
