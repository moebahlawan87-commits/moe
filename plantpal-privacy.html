PlantPal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

PlantPal ("the app") is a plant-care reminder app published by DevMR. In short: your plants are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

Information you create

The plants you add (species, nickname, location, watering schedule) are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used to remind you when a plant needs watering. You can turn these off anytime.
  • Internet / network state — used to load advertisements, the consent form, and (only when you use the Plant Doctor) the optional AI diagnosis feature.
  • Exact alarm & run at boot — used to deliver watering reminders at the right time and to restore them after a restart.

Optional Plant Doctor (AI)

If you choose to use the Plant Doctor, the plant name and symptom text you type are sent to the Pollinations text service to generate advice. This happens only when you tap "Diagnose". No names, emails, account identifiers or your plant list are included. If the service is unavailable, the app shows built-in guidance instead and sends nothing.

Advertising

PlantPal shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), PlantPal uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Ad privacy options.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

PlantPal is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
