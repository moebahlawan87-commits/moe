About this app

SolarSavings USA helps US homeowners understand residential solar. It provides: a lifetime-savings estimator, a system-size + panel-count calculator, an incentives reference (federal ITC + state credits + rebates + net metering + SRECs), a federal ITC explainer with step-down schedule, a payback-period chart with 25-year ROI, a directory of top US installers, a 50-state solar-data reference (sun hours + electric rate + headline incentive), a panel-type comparison, and an AI solar Q&A. Everything except the AI Q&A runs entirely on your device.

AI generation (Pollinations) & PII protection

The AI Q&A sends your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789)
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences (13–19 digits)
  • US phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Postal-style street addresses

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response. AI responses are streamed back to your device and displayed in-app only.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) speak in plain English with US examples (kW system size, panel wattage, the 30% federal ITC, net metering, SRECs, payback), (b) always note that the answer is educational and not installer/tax/financial advice, (c) never invent a binding quote or guaranteed savings figure, (d) ignore any sensitive details a user might include and answer the general question, (e) refuse to help with incentive or tax-credit fraud — inflating system cost, faking install dates, claiming the Residential Clean Energy Credit on a system the user didn't pay for or on a pure rental, or forging invoices (falsely claiming a federal tax credit is tax fraud, 26 U.S.C. § 7201).

How the estimates work

System size is derived from your monthly bill ÷ your state's average electric rate ÷ (state peak-sun-hours × 0.80 derate). Gross cost = system watts × your $/W. The federal ITC is 30% of gross cost. Annual production = system kW × peak sun hours × 365 × 0.80. The 25-year projection applies 3%/yr utility-rate inflation and 0.5%/yr panel degradation. State peak-sun-hours and average residential electric rates are public 2024-2025 aggregate figures (NREL PVWatts ranges, EIA rate data). Headline incentives summarize public DSIRE data. All figures are educational — real numbers depend on your roof orientation, tilt, shading, exact local rate, your utility's net-metering policy, and the installer's actual quote.

Disclaimer (repeated, because it matters)

SolarSavings USA provides educational estimates and AI guidance only. Nothing in the app is a quote, a guarantee of savings, an offer, professional solar/electrical advice, tax advice, or financial advice. The developer is not a licensed solar installer, electrician, tax advisor, or financial advisor. The AI Q&A cannot bind a quote, guarantee your savings, or file your federal tax credit. Always get 2–3 quotes from licensed installers, confirm incentives on DSIREusa.org, and consult IRS.gov or a licensed tax professional about the federal ITC before relying on any figure.

Tax-credit fraud is a federal crime

Falsely claiming the federal Residential Clean Energy Credit — inflating system cost, faking installation dates, claiming the credit on a system you didn't pay for or on a pure rental property, or forging invoices — is tax fraud under 26 U.S.C. § 7201. The AI Q&A refuses these requests by design. To maximize your legitimate benefit, ask about stacking the 30% federal ITC with state rebates, SRECs, net metering, and utility programs.
