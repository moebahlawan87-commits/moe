Reviva — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Reviva ("the app") is a photo enhancement and restoration app published by DevMR. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: your photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

Your photos

When you choose a photo, Reviva reads it through Android's system photo picker — the app only ever receives the single image you pick, and is not granted access to your whole gallery. All enhancement (auto-fix, restore, sharpen, denoise, upscale, colour) runs entirely on your device. Enhanced images are saved only where you choose (your gallery / share sheet). We do not upload, store or transmit your photos.

Optional cloud AI

Reviva ships with on-device processing only. If a future version is configured by the user with a personal AI provider key, an image you explicitly enhance may be sent to that provider solely to return the enhanced result. This is off by default and no key ships with the app.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements and to show the privacy/consent form (and, only if you enable it, the optional cloud AI above).
  • Photos — accessed only via the system photo picker for the single image you select. No broad storage permission is requested.

Advertising

Reviva displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process certain device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Reviva uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request your consent before serving personalised ads. You can change your choice any time via Privacy & ad choices on the home screen.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No access to your location, contacts, or microphone. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Reviva is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date above.
