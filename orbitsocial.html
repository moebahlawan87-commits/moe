Privacy Policy — OrbitSocial

Last updated: 30 May 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is (and is not)

OrbitSocial is an independent hub that helps you jump between the social platforms you already use — Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Snapchat, Telegram, Pinterest, YouTube, WhatsApp, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Reddit, Discord, Threads, Tumblr, and Medium. Each platform tile launches that platform’s own native app if you have it installed, or opens the platform’s public mobile site in your phone’s browser (Chrome Custom Tabs). OrbitSocial never embeds, mirrors, or rewraps any third-party social website inside the app. OrbitSocial is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta, ByteDance, Snap, Telegram, Pinterest, Google, YouTube, X Corp., Microsoft / LinkedIn, Reddit, Discord, Automattic, or Medium. All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

How AI post ideas are generated

When you tap Generate in the Post Ideas (AI) tool, the app sends a short text prompt — built from the platform you chose, the vibe chip you selected, and the topic you typed — to Pollinations’ free public text API at https://text.pollinations.ai/. Pollinations returns 5 caption suggestions which the app shows on screen. No personal information, device identifier, contacts, or list of installed apps is sent.

AI-generated content disclosure

Caption / post suggestions are generated by a third-party large language model. AI tools can be wrong, off-tone, or occasionally suggest content that is not appropriate for your audience. Always review a suggestion before posting it. OrbitSocial includes a Report button on every generated idea (and a global Report option in Settings) so you can flag objectionable AI output to the developer. We also screen prompts client-side to reject obviously disallowed topics before they reach the AI service.

How the hub launcher works

Tapping a platform tile (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, etc.) does one of two things, in order:

  1. If the platform’s native app is installed on your device, OrbitSocial opens that app using a standard Android ACTION_VIEW intent. From that point onward the native app is in charge — not OrbitSocial.
  2. Otherwise, OrbitSocial opens the platform’s public mobile website in your phone’s browser via Chrome Custom Tabs. This is the same browser you would get if you typed the URL yourself; OrbitSocial does not see or store what you do inside that browser tab.

OrbitSocial does not wrap any social platform inside an in-app WebView, does not inject scripts into any platform’s pages, and does not read cookies, session tokens, or content from any third-party site.

Network connections

The Link Vault, Profile Cards, Mindful Mode, and Settings tools all run entirely on your device. The internet is used for: (1) Google AdMob to load ads (see “Advertising” below), and (2) sending the prompt to Pollinations when you tap Generate in Post Ideas (see above). The app requires an active internet connection to open — you will see a “No internet connection” screen when offline.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to request ads and to call the Pollinations text API.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — optional, only used by Mindful Mode to send you a single soft nudge when you reach your daily social-time budget. You can deny this and the rest of the app works normally.
  • Package visibility — for the 15 social apps listed on the hub, Android requires apps that want to query installation status to declare them in the manifest. We only check whether each one is installed; we do not enumerate or transmit your full app list.

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

OrbitSocial shows ads provided by Google AdMob in four formats: banner (small strip at the bottom of most screens), interstitial (occasional full-screen ad after several actions), app-open (full-screen ad when you bring the app back to the foreground), and rewarded (an optional full-screen ad you can choose to watch in exchange for +3 free AI post-idea generations beyond the per-session quota). Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and you can re-open the consent form from Settings → “Manage ad consent”.

Children

OrbitSocial is not designed for children. It launches links to third-party social platforms whose own age ratings vary; users below the age of the platforms they launch should not use those platforms. The AdMob ad content is mixed-audience.
