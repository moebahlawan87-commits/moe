Privacy Policy — Pixora

Last updated: 01 June 2026

The short version: Pixora edits your photos entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The AI Art Generator sends only the text prompt you type — never your photos — to an image-generation provider. Pixora has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics SDK.

About this app

Pixora is an AI photo studio. It enhances, upscales, sharpens, denoises, colour-grades, crops and rotates photos you choose from your device using on-device image processing. It also includes an AI Art Generator that creates a brand-new image from a text description you type.

Your photos stay on your device

All editing — Auto Enhance, HD Upscale, Sharpen, Denoise, the cinematic Looks, and the manual Brightness/Contrast/Saturation/Warmth adjustments — runs locally using your phone's own processor. Your image is never transmitted to Pixora or any third party as part of editing. You pick photos through Android's system Photo Picker, so the app only ever receives the single image you choose — it has no access to the rest of your gallery.

AI Art Generator & prompt safety

When you use the AI Art Generator, the text prompt you type (plus the style and shape you pick) is sent over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-to-image provider, which returns a generated image. We do not send your photos, your name, your device ID, or your advertising ID with the request, and we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the result.

Before a prompt is sent, Pixora applies a safety filter that refuses requests for sexual content, content involving minors, graphic violence, and attempts to deepfake or impersonate real, identifiable people.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required for AI Art generation, the consent form, and ad serving. Pixora shows a “no internet” screen when offline.
  • Write to shared storage (Android 9 and older only) — used solely to save your edited image into the gallery. On Android 10+ this permission is not requested; saving uses scoped storage.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, the full photo library, notifications, SMS, or telephone state.

Advertising

Pixora shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Pixora shows a Google-certified consent form (UMP) the first time you open the app. You can change your choice any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Children

Pixora is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because Pixora stores nothing on any server, there is no account or cloud data to delete. Removing the app from your device removes its local settings. Images you saved to your gallery remain yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer (Developers MR) through the Google Play store listing’s developer-contact section.
