About this app

AutoInsure USA helps US drivers understand car insurance. It provides: a state-aware monthly premium estimator, a coverage-tier comparator, a 50-state minimum-limits reference, an AI claim helper for next-step guidance, an AI policy-clause explainer, a glossary of common auto-insurance terms, and a directory of major US carriers that opens their official quote pages in your browser. Everything except the AI calls runs entirely on your device.

AI generation (Pollinations) & PII protection

The two AI tools (Claim Helper, Policy Explainer) send your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789)
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences (13–19 digits)
  • US phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Postal-style street addresses

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response. AI responses are streamed back to your device and displayed in-app only.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) speak in plain English with US examples, (b) always note that the answer is educational and not insurance or legal advice, (c) never invent specific carrier quotes, premium amounts, or claim numbers, (d) ignore any sensitive details a user might include and answer the general question, (e) refuse to help with insurance fraud — staged crashes, faked injuries, or misleading an adjuster.

Premium estimator — how it works

The estimator runs a small multiplier formula on your device. A national baseline of $150/month for full coverage is multiplied by 2024–2025 publicly-available aggregate factors (state cost of insurance from Bankrate / ZebraInsurance / Insurance.com; demographic and vehicle-class factors from the same public sources). The output is shown as a range of monthly and annual figures, labelled an educational estimate. It is not bound to any insurer’s actual underwriting and your real quote depends on your exact ZIP, credit score (in most states), vehicle VIN and model year, mileage, and the specific carrier.

50-state minimums data source

State minimum required liability limits, no-fault status, and uninsured motorist rules are taken from each state’s Department of Insurance or DMV public summaries, rounded to the standard per-person / per-accident / property-damage reporting format (e.g. 25/50/25 = $25,000 BI per person / $50,000 BI per accident / $25,000 property damage). State laws change — always confirm current minimums with your state DOI before relying on them.
