Privacy Policy — SkyWatch

Last updated: 8 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is

SkyWatch is a stargazing companion. It calculates sunrise/sunset, moon phase and moonrise/set, planet visibility, and a live sky map entirely on your device from your observing coordinates. There are no user accounts and no cloud sync.

What this app collects

SkyWatch does not collect any personal information that identifies you, and your location is never uploaded.

Location (optional)

SkyWatch can use your device’s approximate (coarse) location to set your observing point, but only if you tap “Use my location” and grant the permission. You can instead pick a city or type coordinates by hand — the app works fully without the location permission. When used, your location is processed on-device to calculate sky data and saved locally only; it is never transmitted by the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep SkyWatch free, the app shows advertising through Google’s AdMob SDK (a banner, occasional full-screen ads, and an app-open ad). To serve ads, AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier and IP address. You can reset or limit your advertising ID under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form so you can manage ad choices. Your choice is stored locally by the UMP SDK and respected when loading ads.

Internet required

SkyWatch uses an internet connection to keep the app free with ads. When offline, the app shows a “No internet” screen. The astronomy calculations themselves run entirely on-device.

Children

SkyWatch is a general-audience educational app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
