Zodara — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Zodara ("the app") is a horoscope, tarot & astrology app published by DevMR. In short: your birth details stay only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

For entertainment only

Zodara's horoscopes, tarot readings, compatibility scores and birth-chart estimates are provided for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not advice and are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or psychological guidance.

Information you create

The name, birth date and optional birth time you enter are stored only on your device in the app's private storage and are used to calculate your sun, moon and rising signs. They are never uploaded to us or any server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used to send an optional daily reminder that your reading is ready. You can turn these off anytime in the You tab.
  • Internet / network state — used to load advertisements, the consent form, and (only when you tap "Reveal Deep Reading") the optional AI reading feature.
  • Run at boot — used to restore your daily reminder after the phone restarts.

Optional AI deep reading

If you choose the Deep Cosmic Reading feature, only your zodiac sign and a general theme (e.g. "love", "purpose") are sent to the Pollinations text service to generate a passage. Your name, exact birth date and birth time are never sent. If the service is unavailable, the app generates the reading on your device instead and sends nothing.

Advertising

Zodara shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Zodara uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via You → Ad Privacy Choices.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Zodara is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
