Gulp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Gulp ("the app") is a water-intake tracker published by DevMR. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: your drink log and goal stay on your device.

Information you create

Every drink you log, your daily goal, cup size and reminder settings are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. There is no account system. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to send the optional drink reminders you enable in Settings. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements and to show the privacy/consent form. None of your hydration data is transmitted.
  • Exact alarm & boot — used to deliver reminders on schedule and restore them after the device restarts.

Advertising

Gulp displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process certain device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Gulp uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request your consent before serving personalised ads. You can change your choice any time via Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Not medical advice

Gulp is a convenience tracker, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. Hydration needs vary by person, activity and climate — consult a health professional for guidance.

Children

Gulp is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
