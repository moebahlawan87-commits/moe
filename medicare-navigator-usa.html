About this app

MediCare Navigator USA helps US seniors and caregivers understand Medicare. It provides: a Parts A/B/C/D reference, 5 eligibility paths (age, SSDI, ESRD, ALS, spouse), a 2025 cost reference + IRMAA premium calculator, an Original-vs-Medicare-Advantage comparator, all 6 enrollment windows (IEP/AEP/SEP/GEP/MA-OEP/Medigap-OE), a coverage reference (what's covered, what's not, 5 common surprises), 5 Medicare Advantage plan types (HMO/PPO/PFFS/SNP/MSA), 8 Medigap supplement letter plans, the 6 2025 IRMAA brackets, a ~35-term glossary, and an AI Q&A. Everything except the AI Q&A runs entirely on your device.

AI generation (Pollinations) & PII protection

The AI Q&A sends your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789)
  • Medicare Beneficiary Identifier patterns (e.g. 1AB2-CD3-EF4)
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences (13–19 digits)
  • Long generic IDs / account numbers
  • US phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Postal-style street addresses

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response. AI responses are streamed back to your device and displayed in-app only.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) speak in plain English with US examples (Parts A/B/C/D, Medigap letter plans, IRMAA, IEP/AEP/SEP/GEP), (b) always note that the answer is educational and not insurance/legal/medical advice, (c) never invent specific premiums, plan availability, or coverage decisions for a beneficiary's ZIP, (d) ignore any sensitive details a user might include and answer the general question, (e) refuse to help with Medicare fraud — fake disability, MBI misuse, lying about residency/income, forging records (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b is a federal crime), and direct users to legitimate paths like Medicaid dual-eligibility, Medicare Savings Programs, Extra Help (LIS), and SHIP counseling.

2025 costs & IRMAA data source

Standard 2025 Medicare costs (premiums, deductibles, MOOP cap, Part D out-of-pocket cap) and the 6 IRMAA brackets are taken from the CMS-published 2025 figures. The Part B premium calculator uses these brackets and your input MAGI (modified adjusted gross income). SSA always uses your IRS-reported MAGI from 2 YEARS prior to determine your IRMAA tier — that 2-year lookback is built into the explanation. If you had a Life-Changing Event, the app explains the SSA-44 appeal path. Verify your specific IRMAA on Medicare.gov.

Medicare fraud is a federal crime

Submitting false information to qualify for Medicare benefits, using another person's MBI, lying about residency or income, forging medical records, or any kind of identity misuse is a federal crime under 42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b. The AI Q&A refuses requests for these by design. If you don't think you qualify, ask the AI about legitimate paths instead: Medicaid dual-eligibility, Medicare Savings Programs (QMB / SLMB / QI / QDWI), Extra Help / LIS for Part D, or free SHIP counseling in your state.
