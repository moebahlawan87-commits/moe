Privacy Policy — SquishBlobs

Last updated: 3 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app collects

SquishBlobs does not collect any personal information from you. The app has no user accounts, never asks for your name or email, and does not record your behaviour on a server we control.

How the game works

Every squishy toy is built on your device from code — the 3D models, colours, faces and physics are all generated locally. Nothing about your play is uploaded. Your progress (coins and unlocked skins) is kept only in local storage on your phone and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Network connections

Gameplay runs entirely on your device. The internet is used only by Google AdMob to load ads (see “Advertising” below).

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to request ads.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

SquishBlobs shows ads provided by Google AdMob in four formats: banner (a small strip at the bottom), interstitial (a full-screen ad after some runs), app-open (a full-screen ad when you bring the app back to the foreground), and rewarded (an optional full-screen ad you can choose to watch to double the coins you earned in a run). Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or sets npa=1 (non-personalized ads) so it stays compliant with GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a “family” app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
