Privacy Policy — Fuel Prices World

Last updated: 24 May 2026

What the app does

Fuel Prices World displays indicative reference retail prices for gasoline and diesel across a curated list of countries, intended for educational comparison only. The first launch uses prices bundled with the app. On every subsequent launch the app fetches an updated snapshot from a static JSON file hosted on the publisher's public GitHub Pages site (https://moebahlawan87-commits.github.io/moe/fuel-prices-world.json). The fetch is a plain GET request with no headers, cookies, query parameters, or personal data attached. The snapshot is cached on the device so the app works offline.

The app does not claim to provide real-time pump prices; figures are indicative and actual prices vary by region, brand, time of day, taxes, and currency. Every screen displays the snapshot's "Last updated" date so freshness is always transparent.

Information the app collects

  • No personal information. No name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal data.
  • No account. No sign-in, no profile, no cloud sync.
  • No analytics SDK beyond what Google AdMob requires to serve ads.

Advertising

Fuel Prices World shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. Ads are loaded only when the device has an internet connection.

In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display a consent form on first launch. You can review or change those choices from the "Manage ad privacy choices" button on the About & settings screen.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — to load advertisements served by AdMob and to fetch the prices snapshot.
  • Network state — to detect whether the device is online so the app can show an offline screen instead of failing silently.

The app does not request access to your precise or approximate location.

Nearby gas stations

The "Nearby" tab uses a standard Android geo: intent to open your installed maps application (Google Maps, Waze, OsmAnd, etc.). The maps application handles location lookup under its own privacy policy and permissions. Fuel Prices World never sees your coordinates.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a "family" app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
