How suggestions are generated

When you tap Generate, the app sends a short text prompt — built from the content type you chose (Username / Bio / Caption), the topic or interests you typed, the vibe you selected, and the platform — to Pollinations’ free public text API at https://text.pollinations.ai/openai. Pollinations returns a list of suggestions which the app shows on the Generate screen.

No photo, contact, or device identifier is sent. Only the plain-text prompt you implicitly created by filling in the form is transmitted.

AI-generated content disclosure

Usernames, bios and captions are generated by a third-party large language model. Suggested usernames may already be taken on a given platform, and AI text can be inaccurate, repetitive, or occasionally awkward. Always review a suggestion before using it on a public profile. AI Profile Pro and the developer are not responsible for the content of generated suggestions or for the availability of any specific username on any specific platform.

Network connections

The History tab and Settings run entirely on your device. The internet is used for: (1) Google AdMob to load ads (see “Advertising” below), and (2) sending the prompt to Pollinations when you tap Generate (see above). The app requires an active internet connection to open — you will see a “No internet connection” screen when offline.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to request ads and to call the Pollinations text API.

The app does not request photo, contacts, location, microphone, camera, notifications, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

AI Profile Pro shows ads provided by Google AdMob in four formats: banner (small strip at the bottom of every screen), interstitial (occasional full-screen ad after several generations), app-open (full-screen ad when you bring the app back to the foreground), and rewarded (an optional full-screen ad you can choose to watch in exchange for an extra generation past the per-session free allowance). Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or sets npa=1 (non-personalized ads) so it stays compliant with GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a “family” app on Google Play.
