SubSentry — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

SubSentry ("the app") is a subscription & bill tracker published by DevMR. In short: your subscriptions are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

Information you create

The subscriptions, bills, amounts, dates and notes you enter are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used to alert you before a subscription renews or a free trial ends. You can turn these off anytime.
  • Internet / network state — used to load advertisements, the consent form, and (only when you tap "Get AI tips") the optional AI suggestions feature.
  • Exact alarm & run at boot — used to deliver your renewal and trial reminders at the right time, and to restore them after a restart.

Optional AI money-saving tips

If you choose to use the AI money-saving tips feature, a short anonymous summary of your active subscriptions (service names, categories and monthly amounts) is sent to the Pollinations text service to generate suggestions. This is only sent when you explicitly request tips. No names, emails, payment details or account identifiers are included. If the service is unavailable, the app shows built-in tips instead and sends nothing.

Advertising

SubSentry shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), SubSentry uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Ad privacy options.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

SubSentry is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
