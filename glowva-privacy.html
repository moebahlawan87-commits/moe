Privacy Policy — Glowva

Last updated: 04 June 2026

Important: Glowva is a self-improvement and motivation app. Its routines, challenges, daily plans, and AI Glow Coach are for general wellbeing and lifestyle inspiration only — they are not medical, dietary, dermatological, fitness, or mental-health advice. The app does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. For any health, skin, nutrition, or mental-health concern, please consult a qualified professional. Glowva always promotes healthy, body-positive habits and never extreme dieting or appearance shaming.

About this app

Glowva helps you build a personal "glow-up" routine: morning and evening habit checklists with streaks, a gamified Glow Score and level/XP system, multi-day challenges, unlockable cosmetic "Auras", an AI-generated daily glow-up plan, and an AI Glow Coach for general self-improvement tips on skincare, grooming, fitness, posture, style, confidence, sleep, and social skills. Your progress is stored entirely on your device; only the AI features make a network call.

AI features & safety

The AI Glow Coach and AI daily plan send your typed text over HTTPS to our text-generation providers (Hugging Face inference, with Pollinations as a fallback). We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the request. We have no servers and keep no copy of your prompt or the response; replies are returned to your device and shown in-app only.

Built-in safety: before any prompt is sent, the app screens it on-device. If it detects crisis or self-harm language it does not call the AI — instead it shows supportive helpline resources. If it detects disordered-eating or extreme body-control language it refuses and points to professional support. The AI is also instructed to (a) give only healthy, sustainable, body-positive advice, (b) never recommend extreme dieting, skipping meals, purging, or appearance shaming, (c) never diagnose, prescribe, give dosages, or recommend cosmetic procedures — deferring those to a qualified professional. You can report any objectionable AI output from inside the app (Settings → Report objectionable content, or the flag in the coach).

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, Glowva schedules a gentle local notification at the hour you choose to help keep your streak. This uses an inexact daily alarm on your device; no reminder data leaves the device. On Android 13+ the app asks for notification permission, which you can decline or revoke at any time in system settings. You can switch the reminder off in the app at any time.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — for the AI features, opening this privacy policy, and ad serving.
  • Post notifications (Android 13+) — only if you enable the daily glow reminder.
  • Receive boot completed — to re-arm your daily reminder after the device restarts.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, SMS, or storage.

Advertising

Glowva shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather consent and stays compliant with GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA. You can re-open the privacy-options form any time from Settings. Some optional features (regenerating your AI plan, extra coach messages, early Aura unlocks) are gated behind a rewarded ad.
