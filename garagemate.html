Privacy Policy — GarageMate

Last updated: 8 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is

GarageMate is a private vehicle logbook. You can add your vehicles and record service history, fuel fill-ups (with automatic economy calculation), mileage- and date-based maintenance reminders, and document details such as insurance and registration. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. There are no user accounts and no cloud sync.

What this app collects

GarageMate does not collect any personal information that identifies you, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

Reminders & notifications

Date-based maintenance reminders are scheduled on your device using Android’s alarm and notification system; the content stays on your device. On Android 13+ the app asks for notification permission so it can alert you when service is due.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep GarageMate free, the app shows advertising through Google’s AdMob SDK (a banner, occasional full-screen ads, and an app-open ad). To serve ads, AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier and IP address. You can reset or limit your advertising ID under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form so you can manage ad choices. Your choice is stored locally by the UMP SDK and respected when loading ads.

Internet required

GarageMate uses an internet connection to keep the app free with ads. When offline, the app shows a “No internet” screen. Your logbook data itself does not need the internet and never leaves the device.

Children

GarageMate is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
