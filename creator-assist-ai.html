How suggestions are generated

When you tap Generate, the app sends a short text prompt — built from the creator tool you chose (Hashtags / Hooks / Script / Ideas / Titles), the topic or theme you typed, the niche, and the target platform — to Pollinations’ free public text API at https://text.pollinations.ai/openai. Pollinations returns the suggestions which the app shows on the Generate screen.

No photo, contact, or device identifier is sent. Only the plain-text prompt you implicitly created by filling in the form is transmitted.

AI-generated content disclosure

Hashtags, hooks, scripts, ideas, and titles are generated by a third-party large language model. AI suggestions can be off-trend, repetitive, or already overused. Hashtag pools change daily on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. Always tweak suggestions before posting, follow each platform’s content rules (especially around music licensing, medical / financial / political claims, and sponsored-content disclosure), and add your own creative judgement. Creator Assist AI and the developer are not responsible for content you publish using the app’s output, for engagement results, or for whether a given hashtag, hook, title or script complies with a specific platform’s policies.

Network connections

The History tab and Settings run entirely on your device. The internet is used for: (1) Google AdMob to load ads (see “Advertising” below), and (2) sending the prompt to Pollinations when you tap Generate (see above). The app requires an active internet connection to open — you will see a “No internet connection” screen when offline.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to request ads and to call the Pollinations text API.

The app does not request photo, contacts, location, microphone, camera, notifications, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

Creator Assist AI shows ads provided by Google AdMob in four formats: banner (small strip at the bottom of every screen), interstitial (occasional full-screen ad after several generations), app-open (full-screen ad when you bring the app back to the foreground), and rewarded (an optional full-screen ad you can choose to watch in exchange for an extra batch past the per-session free allowance). Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or sets npa=1 (non-personalized ads) so it stays compliant with GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA.
