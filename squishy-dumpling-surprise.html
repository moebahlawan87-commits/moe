Privacy Policy — Squishy Dumpling Surprise

Last updated: 4 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Squishy Dumpling Surprise ("the App", package com.devmr.squishdumpling) handles information. The App is a single-player blind-box collecting game published by DevMR. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum required to run the game and show ads.

Information the App stores on your device

Your progress — coins, the squishy dumplings & treats you have collected, daily-gift timer, and sound setting — is saved locally on your device only (Android SharedPreferences). We do not upload, sell, or share this data. Removing the App deletes it.

Advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process data such as a device advertising identifier, approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP, and app interaction events. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data (AdMob)

Before any personalized ads are shown in the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland, the App requests consent via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time from the in-app settings.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ad content is requested at a general-audience level.

Permissions

The App requests INTERNET / network-state access solely to load ads. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Data security & retention

Because gameplay data never leaves your device, you remain in control of it. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its own retention policies.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Reopen the consent form from the App's settings to withdraw or change consent.
  • Uninstall the App to erase all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
