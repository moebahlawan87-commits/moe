Privacy Policy — Spelling, Dictionary & Translator

Last updated: 23 May 2026

Summary

The app does not require an account and does not ask you for personal details. Text you enter (for grammar checking, word lookups, or translation) is sent over a secure connection to the relevant third-party service so it can be processed and a result returned. We do not store any of this text on servers we control. The app shows ads through Google AdMob.

What this app collects

The app does not collect any personal information from you. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

What you enter into the app

When you use a feature, the text you type is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the relevant third-party service:

  • Grammar & spell checking — LanguageTool
  • Word definitions & images — Wikimedia (Wiktionary / Wikipedia)
  • Translation — MyMemory (Translated S.r.l.)

We do not store this text on our own servers and we do not link it to your identity. Each service handles the text under its own privacy policy (linked above).

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — required to reach the services above and to load ads.

The app does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDKs may collect and process limited data (for example, your device's advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location) to serve ads and measure ad performance. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a Google-provided consent form (UMP — User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can choose whether ads are personalized.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland you can change your ad-consent choice at any time.
  • You can stop all data processing by not entering text and by uninstalling the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
