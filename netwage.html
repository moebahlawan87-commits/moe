Always confirm with the relevant official tax authority or a qualified adviser before relying on any figure.

About this app

NetWage estimates take-home pay and tax across nine countries (United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Germany, Canada, Ireland, Netherlands, New Zealand and Singapore). It includes a per-country payslip calculator, a side-by-side cross-country comparison, pay-rise / bonus / hourly tools, a plain-English guide and glossary, and an optional AI explainer. Everything except the AI calls and ads runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

AI explainer (Pollinations) & PII protection

The optional “Ask about your pay” tool sends your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • US Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789)
  • UK National Insurance number patterns
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences
  • Email addresses and phone numbers

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response. AI responses are streamed back to your device and displayed in-app only.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) explain in plain English with examples in the relevant country’s currency, (b) always note the answer is a general estimate and the user should confirm with the official tax authority, (c) never invent exact personal figures, and (d) refuse to help with tax evasion, hiding income, or misleading any tax authority.

How the estimates work

NetWage runs small tax formulas entirely on your device. For each country it applies the latest published resident income-tax schedule plus the main employee social contributions (for example FICA in the US, National Insurance in the UK, the Medicare levy in Australia, pension/health/care contributions in Germany, CPP/EI in Canada, USC/PRSI in Ireland, ACC in New Zealand and CPF in Singapore). These are deliberately simplified: they ignore many individual reliefs, regional variations, salary sacrifice, personal tax credits, employer-side costs and in-year rate changes. The cross-country comparison uses approximate, fixed reference exchange rates for illustration only — it is not a live currency conversion. Output is shown as a rounded estimate, not an official figure.

Advertising

NetWage shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather consent, and sets npa=1 (non-personalized ads) by default so it stays compliant with GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA. You can re-open the privacy-options form any time from the Guide tab.
