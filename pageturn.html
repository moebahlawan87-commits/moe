Privacy Policy — PageTurn

Last updated: 8 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is

PageTurn is a personal reading tracker. You can build a library of books, track reading progress, rate finished titles, save quotes and notes, set a yearly reading goal, and keep a reading streak. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. There are no user accounts and no cloud sync.

What this app collects

PageTurn does not collect any personal information that identifies you, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep PageTurn free, the app shows advertising through Google’s AdMob SDK (a banner, occasional full-screen ads, and an app-open ad). To serve ads, AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier and IP address. You can reset or limit your advertising ID under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form so you can manage ad choices. Your choice is stored locally by the UMP SDK and respected when loading ads.

Internet required

PageTurn uses an internet connection to keep the app free with ads. When offline, the app shows a “No internet” screen. Your reading data itself does not need the internet and never leaves the device.

Children

PageTurn is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
