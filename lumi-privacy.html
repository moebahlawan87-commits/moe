Lumi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Lumi ("the app") is a vision-board and manifestation app published by DevMR. In short: your boards, affirmations and goals are stored only on your device.

Information you create

The vision boards, intention tiles and goals you create are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. There is no account. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily affirmation you schedule in Settings. You can turn it off anytime.
  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements and the consent form. None of your boards or goals are transmitted.
  • Exact alarm — used to deliver your daily affirmation at the chosen time.

Affirmations are provided from an on-device library; no text you write is sent anywhere.

Advertising

Lumi shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Lumi uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Privacy & ad settings.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Lumi is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
