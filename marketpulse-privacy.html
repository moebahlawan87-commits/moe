Market Pulse AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Market Pulse AI ("the app") is a market-tracking and briefing app published by DevMR. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: the app keeps your watchlist and alerts on your device and does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Your watchlist and price alerts are stored locally on your device only. We do not have a server, you do not create an account, and this data is never uploaded to us. Removing the app removes this data.

Market data

To show live prices, charts and your daily brief, the app requests publicly available market data from third-party providers — cryptocurrency data from CoinGecko and stock data from Yahoo Finance. These are read-only requests for public quotes; no personal information about you is sent in them. The "AI Market Brief" and per-asset "AI Take" are generated on your device from that public price data. The brief is informational only and is not financial advice.

Price-alert notifications

If you create a price alert, the app periodically checks the relevant public price in the background and shows you a local notification when your target is reached. Notifications are generated on your device; we do not message you from a server.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — to load public market data, advertisements, and the privacy/consent form.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — only so price alerts you set can notify you. You can decline or revoke this at any time in system settings.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier. On first launch you are shown Google's consent form (UMP) where required, and you can reopen your privacy choices from within the app. See Google's partner policy for how ad data is handled.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience interested in financial markets and is not directed at children.
