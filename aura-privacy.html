Aura — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

Aura ("the app") is a personal mood-journal and gratitude app published by DevMR. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: your journal entries and mood data stay on your device.

Information you create

Your daily check-ins — mood, energy, feeling tags, and written reflections — are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to any server. We have no account system and no way to read your entries. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to send the optional daily check-in reminder you schedule in Settings. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements and to show the privacy/consent form. No personal data from your journal is transmitted.
  • Exact alarm — used to deliver your reminder at the time you choose.

Advertising

Aura displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process certain device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Aura uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request your consent before serving personalised ads. You can change your choice any time via Settings → Privacy & ad settings.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Aura is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date above.
