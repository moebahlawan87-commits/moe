Privacy Policy — Envelo

Last updated: 8 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is

Envelo is an envelope-style budget tracker. You create spending “envelopes” with monthly limits, log income and expenses against them, see how much is left to spend, review your history and insights, and set monthly bill reminders. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. There are no user accounts and no cloud sync.

What this app collects

Envelo does not collect any personal information that identifies you, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

Reminders & notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled on your device using Android’s alarm and notification system. The reminder content stays on your device. On Android 13+ the app asks for notification permission so it can alert you at the times you set.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Envelo free, the app shows advertising through Google’s AdMob SDK (a banner, occasional full-screen ads, and an app-open ad). To serve ads, AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier and IP address. You can reset or limit your advertising ID under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form so you can manage ad choices. Your choice is stored locally by the UMP SDK and respected when loading ads. You can reopen these choices any time from Settings > Privacy & ad consent options.

Internet required

Envelo uses an internet connection to keep the app free with ads. When offline, the app shows a “No internet” screen. Your budget data itself does not need the internet and never leaves the device.

Children

Envelo is a general-audience finance utility app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
