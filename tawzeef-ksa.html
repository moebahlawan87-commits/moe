Privacy Policy — Tawzeef KSA

Last updated: 30 May 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is (and is not)

Tawzeef KSA is an independent directory that gathers the top trending Saudi Arabia job-search websites in one place and helps you reach them quickly. It groups portals into Government (Jadarat, Qiwa, Taqat), General boards, Professional, and Gulf & Regional, adds category filters and bookmarks, and includes an offline career guide (CV, interview, and a plain-language explainer of the Saudi labour market). When you tap a job board it opens inside a simple in-app browser, and you can hand the page to your phone’s default browser at any time with “Open in browser”. Tawzeef KSA is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the job boards it links to (Jadarat, Qiwa, Taqat, Bayt, Indeed, LinkedIn, GulfTalent, Naukrigulf, or any other). All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

How the in-app browser works

Tapping a job portal opens that portal’s own public website inside Android’s standard WebView component, scoped to the URL you selected. The page is served directly by the portal — the same content you would get by typing the address into any browser. Tawzeef KSA does not inject scripts into those pages, and does not read your cookies, login credentials, session tokens, or anything you submit on them. For sign-in or applying with an account, we recommend the “Open in browser” button so the flow runs in your full system browser, where the third-party site’s own privacy policy applies. Some portals (for example Jadarat, Qiwa, Indeed and LinkedIn) open directly in your external browser.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to open job portals and to request ads. The app shows a “You’re offline” screen when there is no connection.

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

Tawzeef KSA shows ads provided by Google AdMob in three formats: banner (a small strip above the navigation bar), interstitial (an occasional full-screen ad after several portal opens), and app-open (a full-screen ad when you bring the app back to the foreground — never on the first launch). Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask your choice on first launch.

No fees, ever

Tawzeef KSA is free and will never ask you to pay for a job, a visa, or an application. Legitimate Saudi employers do not charge candidates. Treat anyone who asks for payment as a red flag.

Children

Tawzeef KSA is intended for job seekers and is not designed for children. The AdMob ad content is mixed-audience.
