Privacy Policy — Saleo

Last updated: 6 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is

Saleo is a shopping discount & sales-tax calculator (stacked discounts, deal comparison and unit price). It runs on your device; there are no user accounts and no user-generated content shared with others.

Saleo does not collect personal information that identifies you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Saleo free, the app shows ads via Google’s AdMob SDK, which may process your advertising identifier and IP address. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads; you can reset/limit your ad ID under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

In the EEA, UK, or Switzerland the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to show a consent form so you can manage ad choices; your choice is stored locally and respected when loading ads.
