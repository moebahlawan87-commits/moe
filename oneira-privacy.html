Oneira — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Oneira ("the app") is an AI dream journal published by DevMR. In short: your dreams are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and the dreams you record are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

The dreams you record

The titles, descriptions, moods, lucidity, sleep ratings and tags you enter — together with the reflections Oneira generates from them — are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never transmitted off your device, never sent to a server, and never shared with advertisers or any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes them.

On-device interpretation

Oneira's dream reflections are generated entirely on your device using a built-in symbol lexicon. No dream text leaves your phone for this feature.

Optional AI enhancement

If — and only if — you choose to enter your own free API token in Settings, you may request a richer, AI-written reflection. In that case the text of that dream is sent to the AI text service you have configured to produce the reflection. This feature is off by default; leave the token field blank and Oneira stays fully offline for interpretation.

Voice dictation

The microphone button uses your device's built-in speech recognizer (provided by Android / Google) to turn speech into text. Oneira does not record, store, or transmit audio itself; the system service handles the audio and returns only the recognised text, which is saved locally with your dream.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for the optional morning reminder to capture your dream. You can turn these off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.
  • Internet / network state — used to load advertisements, the consent form, and (only if you enable it) the optional AI enhancement.

Not medical or predictive advice

Oneira's reflections are for personal journaling and self-exploration only. They are not medical, psychological, or predictive advice, and they do not foretell the future. For any health or mental-health concern please consult a qualified professional.

Advertising

Oneira shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob does not receive any of your recorded dreams. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Oneira uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, or photos. We never sell or share personal information, and your dreams never leave your device.

Children

Oneira is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
