Privacy Policy — Knack

Last updated: 6 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is

Knack is a pocket toolbox of twelve precision utilities that read your phone’s built-in hardware sensors: a spirit level, on-screen ruler, protractor, compass, sound meter, metal detector, vibration meter, flashlight with strobe, light meter, stopwatch, tally counter and a device-info panel. Everything runs on your device. There are no user accounts, no chat, and no user-generated content.

What this app collects

Knack does not collect any personal information that identifies you. Sensor readings (tilt, magnetic field, sound level, light level, etc.) are processed live on your device to show you a measurement and are never stored, recorded, or transmitted.

Microphone (Sound Meter only)

The Sound Meter tool reads the microphone to measure how loud your surroundings are. It computes a single loudness value in real time. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or uploaded. The microphone is used only while the Sound Meter is open, and Android shows its microphone-in-use indicator the whole time. Every other tool works without the microphone.

Camera flash (Flashlight only)

The Flashlight tool toggles your device’s camera flash LED. It does not open the camera, take pictures, or record video, and the app does not request the camera permission.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Knack free, the app shows advertising through Google’s AdMob SDK (a banner, occasional full-screen ads, an app-open ad, and an optional “watch a short video” reward to unlock the flashlight strobe). To serve ads, AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier and IP address. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads; you can reset or limit your advertising ID under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form so you can manage ad choices. Your choice is stored locally by the UMP SDK and respected when loading ads. You can reopen these choices from the “Privacy & ad choices” option on the home screen.

Internet required

Knack uses an internet connection to keep the app free with ads. When offline, the app shows a “No internet” screen.

Children

Knack is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
