Privacy Policy — DocuScan Pro

Last updated: 24 May 2026

What this app collects

DocuScan Pro does not collect any personal information from you. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, or precise location.

On-device processing

  • Document capture, cropping, perspective fix, color filters are performed by Google’s ML Kit Document Scanner running on your device.
  • Text recognition (OCR) is performed by Google’s ML Kit Text Recognition running on your device.
  • Read-aloud (TTS) uses Android’s built-in Text-to-Speech engine, which runs on-device.
  • Print hands the PDF to Android’s system print framework, which sends it to the printer (or “Save as PDF”) you pick.

No image, text, or audio leaves your phone for these features.

Network connections

The internet is used only by Google AdMob to load ads (see “Advertising” below). Nothing you scan, type, or extract is sent to any server we control.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — required to capture documents. You grant this at scan time.
  • Internet / network state — required to load ads.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, or broad storage permission. Output files are saved through the Storage Access Framework when you choose to share them.

Advertising

DocuScan Pro shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. Ads are loaded only when the device has an internet connection.

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) on first launch so you can choose whether ads are personalized. You can revisit your choice from Settings → “Manage ad privacy choices”.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a “family” app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
