Privacy Policy — Loan Calculator

Last updated: 23 May 2026

What this app collects

Loan Calculator does not collect any personal information from you. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

What you enter into the app

You enter a principal amount, interest rate, and term to compute a payment schedule. These values stay on your device — they are never sent to any server we control. The app does not provide financial advice.

Network connections

The app uses the internet only to load and show ads (see "Advertising" below). Nothing you enter into the app is sent over the network.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — required to load ads.

The app does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

Loan Calculator shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDKs may collect and process limited data (for example, your device's advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location) to serve ads and measure ad performance. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a Google-provided consent form (UMP — User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can choose whether ads are personalized.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a "family" app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
