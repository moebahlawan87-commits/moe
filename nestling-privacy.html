Nestling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Nestling ("the app") is a baby-care tracking app published by DevMR. It helps parents log feeds, sleep, diapers and growth. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: everything you log stays on your device.

Information you create

Your baby's name, birthday, and every care entry you record — feeds, sleep, diapers and growth measurements — are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to any server. There is no account system and no way for us to read your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to send the optional feed reminder you enable in the Baby tab. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements and to show the privacy/consent form. None of your baby's data is transmitted.
  • Exact alarm & boot — used to deliver your reminder on schedule and to restore it after the device restarts.

Advertising

Nestling displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process certain device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Nestling uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request your consent before serving personalised ads. You can change your choice any time via Baby → Privacy & ad choices.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Not medical advice

Nestling is a personal log for your convenience. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always follow the guidance of your pediatrician or a qualified health professional.

Children

Nestling is intended for parents and caregivers (adults). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
