MealSnap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

MealSnap ("the app") is a recipe & meal-idea app published by DevMR. In short: your pantry, saved recipes and shopping list are stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

Information you create

The ingredients you select, recipes you save and shopping-list items you add are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used to load advertisements, the consent form, and (only when you use the AI Chef) the optional recipe-generation feature.
  • Notifications — optional; used so the app can alert you when a cooking timer finishes.
  • Vibration — used to buzz when a cooking timer ends.

Optional AI Chef

If you tap AI Chef, the list of ingredients you selected is sent to the Pollinations text service to generate a recipe. This happens only when you request it. No names, emails or account identifiers are included. If the service is unavailable, the app shows a built-in recipe instead and sends nothing.

Advertising

MealSnap shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), MealSnap uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Ad privacy options.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

MealSnap is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
