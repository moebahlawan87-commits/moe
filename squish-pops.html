Privacy Policy — Squish Pops

Last updated: 23 May 2026

What this app collects

Squish Pops does not collect any personal information from you. The app does not have user accounts, does not ask for your name or email, and does not record your behavior on a server we control.

Network connections

Squish Pops is a fully offline game — the gameplay loop does not require an internet connection. The internet is used only by Google AdMob to load and display ads. If your device is offline, the game still works; ads simply do not appear.

The app does not send anything you type, anything you tap, or any personal data to any server we control.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required only so that Google AdMob can load ads. The game itself runs offline.

The app does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, notifications, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

Squish Pops shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and app-open formats). Google's advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device's advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), Squish Pops uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) on first launch to show you a consent form. You can choose whether to accept personalized or non-personalized ads. UMP is Google's certified consent solution.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a "family" app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
