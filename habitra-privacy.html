Habitra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

Habitra ("the app") is a habit-tracking RPG published by DevMR. In short: your habits and hero progress are stored only on your device.

Information you create

The habits you add and the days you complete them are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. There is no account and no way for us to read your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily quest reminder you schedule in Settings. You can turn it off anytime.
  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements and the consent form. No habit data is transmitted.
  • Exact alarm — used to deliver your reminder at the chosen time.

Advertising

Habitra shows ads via Google AdMob, including an optional rewarded "treasure chest" you choose to watch. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Habitra uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Privacy & ad settings.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Habitra is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
