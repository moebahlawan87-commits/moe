Privacy Policy — AI Grammar Check

Last updated: 30 May 2026

What this app collects

AI Grammar Check does not collect any personal information from you. There are no user accounts, and the app does not ask for your name or email or record your behavior on a server we control.

Text you submit for checking

To check grammar, define words, or translate, the text you enter is sent over a secure connection to the public language services the app uses (for example the LanguageTool grammar API and dictionary/translation services). Only the text you choose to check is sent — no identifier of you or your device is attached. The text is processed to return corrections and is not used by us to build a profile of you. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information you do not want processed by a third-party service.

Network connections

  • Grammar / dictionary / translation — the text you submit is sent on demand to the relevant public API to return results.
  • Google AdMob — to load and show ads (see “Advertising” below).

Beyond the above, the app does not send your data to any server we control.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required only for the connections listed above.

The app does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, or other sensitive permissions.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — such as your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads, governed by:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or sets npa=1 (non-personalized ads).

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a “family” app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
