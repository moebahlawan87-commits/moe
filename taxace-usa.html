About this app

TaxAce USA helps US individual filers understand federal income tax. It provides: the 2025 federal tax brackets by filing status, a federal income-tax estimator (with marginal + effective rate + FICA), a standard-vs-itemized deduction reference, 9 federal tax credits (CTC, EITC, AOTC, LLC, Saver's, PTC, Clean Energy, EE Home Improvement, Clean Vehicle), a refund/balance-due estimator, a 50-state income-tax rate reference (including 9 no-tax states), a Schedule C self-employed deductions reference, a 2026 tax-deadline calendar, a ~36-term plain-English glossary, and an AI tax Q&A (PII-redacted, refuses tax fraud). Everything except the AI Q&A runs entirely on your device.

AI generation (Pollinations) & PII protection

The AI Q&A sends your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789)
  • Employer Identification Number patterns (e.g. 12-3456789)
  • ITIN patterns
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences (13–19 digits)
  • US phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Postal-style street addresses

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response. AI responses are streamed back to your device and displayed in-app only.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) speak in plain English with US examples (1040, brackets, standard deduction, itemized, federal credits, Schedule C, IRS payment plans), (b) always note that the answer is educational and not tax/legal/financial advice, (c) never invent specific refund amounts or IRS rulings for a filer's situation, (d) ignore any sensitive details a user might include and answer the general question, (e) refuse to help with tax evasion, fake deductions/credits/dependents, forged W-2s/1099s, underreported tips, or offshore concealment (26 U.S.C. § 7201 is a federal crime), and direct users to legitimate paths like the standard deduction, Saver's Credit, EITC, IRS payment plans, or free VITA/TCE filing help.

2025 tax data source

Federal tax brackets, standard deduction, federal credits, IRMAA-style surcharges (not modeled here), and Schedule C line numbers are taken from IRS-published 2025 figures, Form 1040 + Schedule C + Schedule A + relevant credit forms, and IRS Pub 17. State income-tax rates are the top marginal rates published by each state's Department of Revenue (2024-2025 cycle). All figures are educational — verify specifics on IRS.gov or the state DOR.

Tax evasion is a federal crime

Hiding income, inflating business expenses, fabricating charitable contributions, claiming dependents who aren't your qualifying child/relative, forging W-2s or 1099s, underreporting cash tips, or using offshore accounts to conceal income is a federal crime under 26 U.S.C. § 7201. The AI Q&A refuses requests for these by design. If you don't think you can afford your tax bill, ask the AI about legitimate paths instead: the standard deduction, EITC, Saver's Credit, IRS Direct Pay, IRS installment plans (Form 9465), Offer in Compromise, or free VITA / TCE assistance.
