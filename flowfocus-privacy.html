FlowFocus — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

FlowFocus ("the app") is a focus-timer app published by DevMR. In short: your focus sessions and world are stored only on your device.

Information you create

Your focus sessions and the world you grow are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or any server. There is no account. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily focus reminder you schedule in Settings. You can turn it off anytime.
  • Internet / network state — used only to load advertisements and the consent form. No session data is transmitted.
  • Exact alarm — used to deliver your reminder at the chosen time.

Ambient soundscapes are synthesised on-device and require no microphone or audio-recording permission.

Advertising

FlowFocus shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), FlowFocus uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Privacy & ad settings.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

FlowFocus is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
