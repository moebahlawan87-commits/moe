Privacy Policy — Dubai Jobs Hub

Last updated: 30 May 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is (and is not)

Dubai Jobs Hub is an independent directory that gathers the top trending Dubai & UAE job-search websites in one place and helps you reach them quickly. On top of that directory the app adds its own native features: sector filters, a search box, bookmarks, and an offline job-seeker toolkit (CV, interview, documents and rights guidance). When you tap a job board, it opens inside a simple in-app browser, and you can hand the page off to your phone’s default browser at any time with “Open in browser”. Dubai Jobs Hub is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the job boards it links to (Bayt, GulfTalent, Naukrigulf, Indeed, LinkedIn, Dubizzle, Dubai Careers, DEWA, Michael Page, or any other). All product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

How the in-app browser works

Tapping a job board opens that board’s own public website inside Android’s standard WebView component, scoped to the URL you selected. The page is served directly by the job board — the same content you would get by typing the address into any browser. Dubai Jobs Hub does not inject scripts into those pages, and does not read your cookies, login credentials, session tokens, or the content you submit on them. For sign-in or applying with an account, we recommend using the “Open in browser” button so the flow runs in your full system browser, where the third-party site’s own privacy policy applies.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to load job-board pages and to request ads. The app shows an “You’re offline” screen when there is no connection.

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, camera, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

Dubai Jobs Hub shows ads provided by Google AdMob in four formats: banner (a small strip above the navigation bar), interstitial (an occasional full-screen ad after several portal opens), app-open (a full-screen ad when you bring the app back to the foreground — never on the first launch), and rewarded (an optional full-screen ad you choose to watch in the Toolkit to permanently unlock the bonus “Salary & negotiation” guide). Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and you can re-open the consent form from More → “Ad privacy / consent options”.

No fees, ever

Dubai Jobs Hub is free and will never ask you to pay for a job, a visa, or an application. Legitimate UAE employers do not charge candidates. Treat anyone who asks for payment as a red flag — this guidance is also built into the in-app toolkit.

Children

Dubai Jobs Hub is intended for job seekers and is not designed for children. The AdMob ad content is mixed-audience.
