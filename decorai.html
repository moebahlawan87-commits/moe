Privacy Policy — DecorAI

Last updated: 03 June 2026

The short version: DecorAI’s Style Finder, Palette Studio and Moodboard run entirely on your device. Photos you pick for palette extraction are never uploaded. The AI Designer and AI Advisor send only the text you provide (room type, style, color hex codes and any notes — never your photos) to an AI provider over HTTPS. DecorAI has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics SDK.

About this app

DecorAI is an interior-design helper. It generates photorealistic room design concepts from a text description you build, extracts color palettes from a photo on your device, runs a style-finder quiz, offers an AI design advisor, and lets you save ideas to an on-device Moodboard.

Your photos stay on your device

Palette extraction runs locally using your phone’s own processor — your image is never transmitted to DecorAI or any third party. You pick photos through Android’s system Photo Picker, so the app only ever receives the single image you choose; it has no access to the rest of your gallery. The Style Finder quiz, the style library and the Moodboard work fully offline.

AI Designer & AI Advisor

When you generate a room concept or ask the advisor a question, the text you supply (room type, style, palette hex codes, and any notes or question you type) is sent over HTTPS to our AI provider — Hugging Face Inference and/or Pollinations — which returns a generated image or text answer. We do not send your photos, your name, your device ID or your advertising ID with these requests, and we do not keep a copy of the request or the result.

Before anything is sent, DecorAI applies a safety filter that refuses requests for sexual content, content involving minors, graphic violence, and off-topic harmful prompts. Generated concepts are creative design ideas, not architectural or construction plans.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required for AI generation, the consent form and ad serving. The AI screens show an offline notice when there is no connection.
  • Write to shared storage (Android 9 and older only) — used solely to save a design concept into your gallery. On Android 10+ this permission is not requested; saving uses scoped storage.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, the full photo library, notifications, SMS or telephone state.

Advertising

DecorAI shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), DecorAI shows a Google-certified consent form (UMP) the first time you open the app. You can change your choice any time from Settings → Privacy options.

Children

DecorAI is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because DecorAI stores nothing on any server, there is no account or cloud data to delete. Removing the app from your device removes its local settings, saved palettes and concepts. Images you saved to your gallery remain yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer (Developers MR) through the Google Play store listing’s developer-contact section.
