About this app

Aegis Insurance USA helps US consumers learn about insurance through five educational premium estimators (auto, home, health, life, renters), an AI policy explainer, an AI claim-helper, an insurance glossary, and a side-by-side policy comparator. All estimators run entirely on your device.

AI generation (Pollinations) & PII protection

The four AI tools (Policy Explainer, Claim Helper, Glossary deep-dive, Compare) send your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789 or 9 consecutive digits)
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences (13–19 digits)
  • Account-number-length digit sequences (10+ consecutive digits)
  • Email addresses
  • Driver’s-license-style identifiers (e.g. a letter followed by 7–12 digits)

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) answer in educational tone with US examples, (b) always append the disclaimer that the answer is not insurance advice, (c) never invent specific carrier quotes or claim numbers, (d) ignore any sensitive details a user might include and answer the general question, (e) refuse to help with insurance fraud.

Estimators — how they work

Each estimator runs a small regression formula on your device using your inputs and the catastrophe/demographic factor for the US state you selected. The output is rounded to a monthly and annual figure and labelled as an educational estimate. Numbers are derived from publicly available 2024 averages (NAIC, Insurance Information Institute, Kaiser Family Foundation). They are not bound to any insurer’s rates.
