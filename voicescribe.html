Last updated: 24 May 2026

What this app collects

VoiceScribe does not collect any personal information from you. No accounts, sign-in, profile, or analytics.

On-device processing

Recording uses Android’s built-in MediaRecorder (AAC audio in MP4 container, 96 kbps mono). Audio is saved to the app’s private storage and is never uploaded to any server we control.

Network connections

The internet is used only by Google AdMob to load ads (see “Advertising” below). Your voice recordings are never transmitted.

Permissions the app requests

  • Record audio — required to capture voice memos. Requested at runtime the first time you tap Record.
  • Internet / network state — required to load ads.

The app does not declare a foreground-microphone service, does not record in the background, and does not request location, contacts, camera, or broad storage permission.

Advertising

VoiceScribe shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. Ads are loaded only when the device has an internet connection.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a “family” app on Google Play.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
