About this app

DebtZero USA helps US consumers plan getting out of debt. It provides: a debt tracker, a debt-payoff planner that compares the snowball and avalanche methods with extra-payment projections, a credit-utilization calculator with score-factor education, a consolidation comparator (consolidation loan vs. 0% balance transfer), an AI debt coach for general guidance, and a plain-English glossary. Every calculation runs entirely on your device; only the AI coach makes a network call.

AI generation (Pollinations) & PII protection

The AI tools send your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789)
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences (16 digits)
  • US phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Postal-style street addresses

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response. AI responses are returned to your device and displayed in-app only.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) speak in plain English with US examples, (b) always note that the answer is educational and not financial, credit, or legal advice, (c) favor proven, free strategies and warn about scams (advance-fee “credit repair”, CPNs, harmful debt-settlement), (d) ignore any sensitive details a user might include and answer the general question, (e) refuse to help with credit or lending fraud — hiding assets from creditors, faking income, synthetic-identity / CPN schemes, or deceiving a lender.

Payoff & calculators — how they work

The payoff planner simulates your debts month by month on your device: it accrues monthly interest from the APR you enter, applies your minimum payments, and funnels any extra payment into the priority debt (highest APR for avalanche, smallest balance for snowball), rolling each freed payment forward. The credit and consolidation tools use standard amortization and utilization formulas. All outputs are educational estimates based solely on the figures you enter — your real results depend on your actual balances, rates, fees, and payment timing.
