Lunaria — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Lunaria ("the app") is a period & menstrual-cycle tracker published by DevMR. In short: your health data is stored only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and your cycle information is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

The health data you log

The period dates, flow, symptoms, moods and notes you enter — together with the predictions Lunaria calculates from them — are stored only on your device in the app's private storage. This is sensitive personal information and we treat it that way: it is never transmitted off your device, never sent to a server, and never shared with advertisers or any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to remind you before a predicted period. You can turn these off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.
  • Internet / network state — used to load advertisements and the consent form. Your cycle data is not sent over the internet.

Not medical advice

Lunaria's predictions are estimates for personal awareness only. They are not medical advice and not a method of contraception. Cycles vary; for any medical concern please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Advertising

Lunaria shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob does not receive any of your logged health data. Google may process device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by law (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA), Lunaria uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before serving personalised ads. Change your choice anytime via Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Data we do NOT collect

No accounts. No analytics SDKs. No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos. We never sell or share personal information, and your health data never leaves your device.

Children

Lunaria is intended for people who menstruate and is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
