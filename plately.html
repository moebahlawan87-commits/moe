Privacy Policy — Plately

Last updated: 8 June 2026 (v1.0)

What this app is

Plately is a calorie & macro food diary. You log the foods you eat each day against a calorie goal, track carbs, protein and fat, log water and weight, and review your trends. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. There are no user accounts and no cloud sync.

What this app collects

Plately does not collect any personal information that identifies you, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

Health & nutrition information

The calorie and macro figures Plately calculates are estimates for general informational purposes only. Plately is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet, especially if you have a medical condition.

Reminders & notifications

The optional daily logging reminder is scheduled on your device using Android’s alarm and notification system. The reminder content stays on your device. On Android 13+ the app asks for notification permission so it can alert you at the time you set.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Plately free, the app shows advertising through Google’s AdMob SDK (a banner, occasional full-screen ads, and an app-open ad). To serve ads, AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier and IP address. You can reset or limit your advertising ID under Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

EEA / UK / Switzerland consent (UMP)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form so you can manage ad choices. Your choice is stored locally by the UMP SDK and respected when loading ads. You can reopen these choices any time from the app’s settings.

Internet required

Plately uses an internet connection to keep the app free with ads. When offline, the app shows a “No internet” screen. Your diary data itself does not need the internet and never leaves the device.

Children

Plately is a general-audience health & fitness utility app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
