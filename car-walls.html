Wallpapers shipped with the app

The Featured and Brands tabs show artwork that was AI-generated at build time and bundled inside the app. These wallpapers are loaded from local files on your device — no network call is made when you browse the gallery or apply one of them as a wallpaper.

The AI artwork is stylised: it does not use any real photograph or trademarked logo from any car manufacturer. CarWalls is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any car manufacturer or brand named in the catalogue. Brand names are used only to indicate the styling theme of the AI artwork.

AI Search (Pollinations.AI)

The AI Search tab lets you type a car-related prompt and generate a fresh wallpaper. When you tap Generate (or a suggestion chip), the app sends:

  • your typed prompt (with a fixed style suffix appended) to https://image.pollinations.ai/prompt/…;
  • standard HTTP request headers added by the Android networking stack (e.g. your IP address, used by Pollinations for rate-limiting).

Your prompt history is not stored anywhere — not on your device, not on a CarWalls server. Pollinations.AI’s handling of the data they receive is governed by Pollinations’s site terms. The returned image is held in your phone’s memory while you preview it; tapping Apply hands it to Android’s WallpaperManager — the image is not re-uploaded anywhere.

Network connections

All wallpaper rendering and applying runs on your device. The internet is used for: (1) Google AdMob to load ads (see “Advertising” below), and (2) when you use the AI Search tab, sending your typed prompt to the Pollinations.AI image service. The app requires an active internet connection to open — you will see a “No internet connection” screen when offline.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet / network state — required to request ads and to call Pollinations.AI from the AI Search tab.
  • Set wallpaper — required to install your chosen wallpaper on the Home / Lock screen via Android’s system WallpaperManager.
  • Write external storage (Android 9 and below only, android:maxSdkVersion="28") — legacy permission required only on older Android versions for the wallpaper decode path.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, notifications, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

CarWalls shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded formats). The rewarded format is used to gate AI Search generations — you choose to watch a short ad in order to generate an AI wallpaper. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or sets npa=1 (non-personalized ads) so it stays compliant with GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not classified as a “family” app on Google Play.
