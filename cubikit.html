Privacy Policy — Cubikit

Last updated: 23 May 2026

What this app collects

Cubikit does not collect any personal information from you. The app does not have user accounts, does not ask for your name or email, does not connect to the internet, and does not record your behavior on a server we control.

Network connections

Cubikit is a fully offline game. The app does not request the internet permission and does not communicate with any server. It cannot send data even if it wanted to.

Permissions the app requests

Cubikit requests no Android runtime permissions. Specifically, it does not request internet, network state, location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, notifications, or any other sensitive permission.

Advertising

The current version of Cubikit contains no ads and no third-party advertising SDK. If a future version introduces ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's permissions and the Google Play data-safety form will be updated accordingly.

Children

Cubikit is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data, has no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no internet connection, it imposes no risk of personal-data exposure for children.

Clearing your data

You can clear the saved world at any time by tapping Reset inside the app, or by clearing app storage from Android Settings → Apps → Cubikit → Storage. Uninstalling the app removes the save file automatically.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
