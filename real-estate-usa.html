About this app

RealEstate USA helps US consumers learn about homebuying through five on-device calculators (Mortgage, Affordability, Rent vs Buy, Closing Costs, Home Equity & HELOC) and a plain-English AI Realtor that answers general questions about US real estate. All calculators run entirely on your device.

AI generation (Pollinations) & PII protection

The AI Realtor sends your typed question over HTTPS to Pollinations, our text-generation provider. Before sending, the app automatically scrubs:

  • Social Security number patterns (e.g. 123-45-6789)
  • Credit-card-length digit sequences (13–19 digits)
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Routing / account number patterns

We do not attach your name, device ID, advertising ID, or any account identifier to the AI request. We have no servers; we do not keep a copy of the prompt or the response.

System prompt: the AI is instructed to (a) answer in plain English with US examples, (b) always lead with the disclaimer that the answer is educational only and not legal/tax/financial advice, (c) refuse and call out any request that discriminates by race / religion / national origin / sex / familial status / disability (Fair Housing Act), any request to falsify income or employment (loan fraud), and any predatory practice, (d) never invent specific lender names, current rates, or guaranteed approval odds.

Calculators — how they work

Each calculator runs a small formula on your device using your inputs and educational state-level factors (median home price, effective property tax rate, typical homeowners-insurance cost per $1,000 of dwelling coverage, and typical buyer-side closing percent). State factors are rounded approximations sourced from public 2024–2025 averages (US Census ACS, Federal Reserve, Tax Foundation, NAIC, MBA). They are not bound to any lender’s actual rates, taxes, or insurance quotes. Output is rounded to whole-dollar monthly and total figures and labelled as an educational estimate.

RealEstate USA shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and processes limited data — for example, your device’s advertising identifier and approximate IP-based location — to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

If you live in a region where consent is required (the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy law), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather consent, and sets npa=1 (non-personalized ads) by default so it stays compliant with GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA. You can re-open the privacy-options form any time from Settings.
